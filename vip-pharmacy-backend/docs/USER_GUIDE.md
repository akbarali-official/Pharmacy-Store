# Daily user guide

## Opening the day (cashier)
1. Sign in → *Cash & Expenses* → **Open session** and enter the cash in the drawer.

## Selling
1. *POS / Billing* → search by name, generic name, barcode or SKU (`F2` jumps to search, `Enter` adds the first result).
2. Optionally choose a customer, set a discount and payment method.
3. Prescription medicines show **Rx** — check the prescription and tick the confirmation.
4. **Complete sale (F9)** → print the receipt.
5. Use **Hold** to park a bill while serving another customer.

## Returns and refunds
*Sales & Returns* → open the bill → enter quantities → choose refund method → **Record return**. Large refunds need a manager.

## Receiving stock
1. *Suppliers & Purchases* → **New purchase**: supplier, medicine, batch number, expiry, quantity, cost → **Save draft**.
2. When the goods arrive click **Receive**. Stock and the supplier balance update at that moment.
3. Pay suppliers in *Supplier Accounts* → **Record payment**.

## Keeping stock healthy
- Dashboard **Needs attention** lists expired, expiring and low-stock items.
- *Business Tools → Reorder list* shows what to buy; *Expiry report* shows what to return or discount.
- Wrong count? *Inventory → Batches → Adjust* (a reason is required and recorded).

## Closing the day
1. *Cash & Expenses* → count the drawer → **Close session** (variance is recorded).
2. *Business Tools → Daily closing* → print the report.
3. Take a backup.

## Setting up a new store quickly
*Import & Export* → download the template → fill it in Excel → save as CSV → **Check file** → **Import now**.
